# FairShift — two-minute demo script

**0:00–0:15 · Landing and sector context** — On the landing page, point out the SDG 8 impact strip, the worker/admin demo buttons and the short guided tour. Open **How FairShift works across sectors** to show that the safety labels and location language change for Construction and College-Campus teams.

**0:15–0:50 · Worker, Construction** — Choose **Try Worker Demo**. On the mobile-style home, check the shift and weekly hours. Start **Check in**, use **Simulate QR** if a camera is not available, and note that location is optional. Open **Pay** to show base, overtime, deductions and net pay; press **Listen** or download the CSV. Open **Safety**, choose a hazard, add a short report and view the status tracker. Finish on **Wellbeing** to demonstrate the private mood check-in and water/break cue.

**0:50–1:05 · Worker, Campus** — Use the floating **Demo Switcher** and choose Worker + Campus. Show the campus organization label, department-style shift location and campus hazards (wet floors, lab safety, fire exits). Point out the persistent voice/help button and language selector.

**1:05–1:35 · Admin, Construction** — Switch to Admin + Construction. Start with attendance and overtime trend cards, then explain one heatmap cell. Open the fatigue watchlist and show how reasons are visible. Scroll to the attrition heuristic and suggested actions. Open Safety Reports, assign one report, add a short operational comment and move its status forward.

**1:35–1:50 · Feedback privacy and skills** — Open Feedback to show thresholded, team-level aggregates only; scores and themes stay hidden below five usable responses. Open Skills & Training to add an entry or verify a reviewed credential.

**1:50–2:00 · Admin, Campus and install/offline** — Switch to Admin + Campus to show the same reporting tools with campus terminology and safety categories. Close by pointing out the installable FairShift shell, dark-mode toggle and online/offline status; previously viewed information is cached, and queued actions sync only for the matching signed-in demo identity.

## Demo accounts

- Construction worker: `worker.build` / `ShiftReady8!`
- Construction admin: `admin.build` / `FairTeam8!`
- Campus worker: `worker.campus` / `ShiftReady8!`
- Campus admin: `admin.campus` / `FairTeam8!`

The floating Demo Switcher is the quickest way to change views during a review. Demo QR scans use the selected sector's simulated value; camera and browser speech features depend on device/browser permissions.
